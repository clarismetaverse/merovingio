# Multiple useful bridges and objective-relative equivalence

Parent 0bb6e67, branch codex/multi-bridge-relevance. Main, v0.9/v1.0 and prior experiments preserved. Jev 1.14.0, only Actions using TYPESAFE_API_KEY secret and TypeSafe-Danger-Zone-Images: true. No long context, automatic retries or repair questions.

## Prespecified small experiment

One isosceles host with base endpoints A=(-110,75), B=(110,75), apex C=(0,-65). Three medians: each original vertex joins the midpoint of its opposite side. Every candidate passes the existing material/convex-polygon checks for both historical local GS contracts: adjacent triangles using the same complete material side, and a shared carrier with host-partition participation. Inline certificates include objective-independent metric facts computed from the fixture. They are deliberately supplied so Jev rates pertinence rather than reconstructing bindings, distances or proofs.

Use two presentations, original and 37-degree rotation plus reflection, with randomized labels/candidate order inherited from the previous pilot. Within a presentation only active_objective changes. The same candidates, images, metric facts, certificates, questions and criteria are reused for all three objectives. Across studies these aliases are intentionally reused; no comparison to the preceding scalene geometry is treated as causal evidence.

1. Intact anchor: prepare a later comparison retaining the entire designated reference side in a subfigure, without preferring either endpoint. Both base-end medians satisfy this; the apex median splits the designated side. The base-end medians are related by an explicitly checked reflection of this SAME host. Reference tiers: {from_A,from_B} > {from_C}.
2. Unanchored decomposition: organize two adjacent triangular views through their common material interface, ignoring original vertex/side, orientation and size. All three satisfy the same role structure. They are equivalent under this objective, despite differing metric details. This broad all-useful control tests unwarranted differentiation rather than difficult semantic inference. One tier: {from_A,from_B,from_C}.
3. Exchangeable views: exchange the two subfigures by a symmetry of the fixed host without changing their metric shapes. Only the apex median supplies this; the two base-end medians are equivalent lower alternatives. Check both side-length congruence and invariance of the cut under the host reflection. All three medians give equal areas: equal areas alone must not certify congruence. Reference tiers: {from_C} > {from_A,from_B}.

Objectives are task descriptions, not references to correct candidate aliases. Global equivalence groups, target-match flags, useful-set cardinalities, reference tiers, ranks, numeric score targets and selection parameters remain observer-only. Candidate certificates can legitimately expose congruence facts independent of the objective; the exchange case is therefore a selective control with explicit evidence, not a test of hidden symmetry discovery. The reference is an operational task policy, without external human judgments of mathematical usefulness.

## Questions and scoring

Three candidates x two axes x three objectives x two presentations = 36 native Score answers in six serial six-question requests. Axes: relevance to active Mental Spaces and usefulness of certified Generic Spaces for the requested comparison. They are correlated views. Four descriptive levels each, independent per candidate; several or all candidates can receive the same high level. No Choice or requirement to distribute a score budget.

Preserve native score/probabilities/confidence/reliability or null and usage; normalize score by three. Fixed composite .5/.5. Raw argmax and exact numerical ties are shown but are not the success criterion.

## Equivalence, partial orders and set policy, fixed before live

Practical equivalence tolerance delta=.10 on normalized composites (native score gap .30), applied to pairs declared equivalent by the reference policy. This is an engineering tolerance, not a calibrated statistical equivalence test. Report every pair gap, tier spread and per-axis result. There are ten equivalent pairs across both presentations, eight of them inside the useful tiers; the other two compare the lower base-end alternatives in the exchange case. Report eight strict preference pairs, their signs, ties and whether margins exceed delta. These comparisons are correlated.

An illustrative downstream policy retains every candidate with composite >= 2/3 AND within delta of the maximum. Empty and multi-candidate outputs are allowed. The floor corresponds to the descriptive supported/partly supported region of the scale; it is an ex ante operational choice, not a calibrated probability or deployment guarantee. Report exact useful-set matches, TP/FP/FN, empty sets and spurious one-element sets when multiple candidates are expected. Also report the predeclared delta=.05,.10,.15 settings together. Never select a tolerance after seeing results or alter native scores to restore reference equivalence.

Expected useful memberships total twelve across six groups. A policy selecting all candidates always would recover only the two all-useful groups, with six false positive memberships. A forced single winner cannot exactly recover either of the four multi-useful groups. No aggregate top-1 accuracy is claimed.

## Validation and limits

Before live, test both contracts, geometry/material mutations, metric profiles versus area equality, reflection isometry and cut mapping, objective-only counterfactuals, non-leakage, 36-question budget, set boundaries, practical ties, partial-order violations, transformations, Actions guard and offline no-result behavior. Run inherited suites, standard viewer build and request/result audit on branch. Reconstruct actual requests and image hashes after download; recompute reference tiers and analysis offline. Save protocol, report, native responses, failures, usage, images, viewer and reproducibility hashes in outputs and Library.

This tests explicit-goal relevance on supplied certified facts in one symmetric synthetic fixture. It does not test free Generic Space discovery, theorem proof, autonomous pixel interpretation or general mathematical creativity. There is no uncertified arm or causal comparison with earlier benchmarks. The images are prospective alternatives, not temporal frames or evidence of sequential internal reasoning.
